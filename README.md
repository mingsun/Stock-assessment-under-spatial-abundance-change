Code for Sun et al. (2026)

This repository contains the analysis code associated with:

Sun, M., Bolser, D., Blaylock, J., Chen, Y., Hennen, D., & Truesdell, S. (2026).
Offshore wind farm-induced spatial redistribution of abundance alters survey indices and stock assessment performance.
ICES Journal of Marine Science, 83(8), fsag164.
https://doi.org/10.1093/icesjms/fsag164

Overview

This study evaluates how offshore wind farm-induced spatial redistribution of marine species may interact with survey preclusion and affect fishery-independent survey indices and stock assessment performance in the U.S. Mid-Atlantic.

Three case-study stocks were examined:

Summer flounder (Paralichthys dentatus)
Longfin squid (Doryteuthis pealeii)
Atlantic surfclam (Spisula solidissima)

The repository contains code for:

Processing fishery-independent survey and offshore wind spatial data
Simulating attraction- and production-driven abundance redistribution
Creating Full and Wind Energy Excluded (WEE) survey scenarios
Calculating and comparing survey abundance indices
Propagating modified indices through stock assessment models
Evaluating assessment estimates, residual patterns, and retrospective performance
Producing manuscript figures and supporting analyses
Data

The analyses use NOAA Northeast Fisheries Science Center fishery-independent survey data, associated stock assessment models, and BOEM Wind Energy Area spatial data.

Raw data and assessment input files may not all be included in this repository and should be obtained from the original data providers where applicable.

Software

Analyses were conducted primarily in R.
